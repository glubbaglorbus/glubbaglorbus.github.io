Lego Instructions Project

The Lego Instructions Project is a document to give step by step hardware-akin instructions on how to build our custom Lego build

  • Skills used: Document Writing
  • My role: Document Writer and Revisor
  • Challenge we encountered:Making sure the document was easy to understand for someone unfamiliar with the subject
  1. SSituation The assignment was to create detailed instructions on how to build a Lego structure
  2. TTask Document writing, revision and teamwork
  3. AActions I had to take into account that the user may be unfamiliar, so when writing instructions I had to be very thorough with piece names and placement
  4. RResults We acheived a high grade on this assignment and went above what was required for this

Reflection

It helped me to improve on rereading and following instructions, in a situation where I may have to guide a new hire at work it could prove useful. Alternatively, helped me to understand how to guide someone who may be unfamilair with a subject

Project Proposal

It was a 57-page Mock Hardware Proposal for a company, in which we had to come up below a budget and meet all the requirements for all 250 computers. As well as methods for data backup, installation and disposal of old parts. We ended up receiving a best-in-class award for our efforts.

  • SKills used:Document writing, Attentative Research and Budgeting
  • My role:Research
  • Challenge I solved: Due to AI and Data Centers shooting RAM prices through the roof, the budget for this assignment was outdated. So I had to do heavy researhc into compatable and readily available parts for sale which met the requirements while still working together with underpowered RAM
  1. SSituation It was written and researched for a fictional company (BTB Heavy Equipment)
  2. TTask Researching the hardware components and their compatabiltiy
  3. AActions [The steps YOU took: research, writing, planning the schedule and budget, presenting, revising after feedback.] Researching inital parts and rescoping my choices based on budgeting issues. Where was I over? Where can I splurge?
  4. RResults We ended up winning a best in class award and achieved a 96%

Reflection

It was helpful in improving my research capabailities and tracking down the best attainable parts and prices. Showing I was able to track down and jot the best parts including pictures, availabilty and price
