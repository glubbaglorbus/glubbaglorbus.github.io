Lego Instructions Project

The Lego Instructions Project is a document that provides step-by-step hardware-style instructions on how to build our custom Lego model.

  • Skills used: Document Writing
  • My role: Document Writer and Revisor
  • Challenge we encountered: Making sure the document was easy to understand for someone unfamiliar with the subject
  1. SSituation The assignment was to create detailed instructions on how to build a Lego structure
  2. TTask Document writing, revision, and teamwork
  3. AActions I had to take into account that the user may be unfamiliar with the subject, so I ensured the instructions were thorough and clearly explained piece names and placement.
  4. RResults We achieved a high grade on this assignment and exceeded the project requirements.

Reflection

It helped me improve my ability to reread and follow instructions. In situations where I may need to guide a new hire at work, this experience could prove useful. Additionally, it helped me understand how to guide someone who may be unfamiliar with a subject.

Project Proposal

A 57-page mock hardware proposal for a company in which we had to stay within budget while meeting all requirements for 250 computers, as well as methods for data backup, installation, and disposal of old parts. We ended up receiving a best-in-class award for our efforts.

  • Skills used: Document Writing, Attentive Research, and Budgeting
  • My role: Research
  • Challenge I solved: Due to AI and data centers driving RAM prices higher, the budget for this assignment was outdated. I had to conduct extensive research into compatible and readily available parts that met the requirements while remaining within budget.
  1. SSituation It was written and researched for a fictional company (BTB Heavy Equipment)
  2. TTask Researching hardware components and their compatibility
  3. AActions Researching initial parts and revising my choices based on budget constraints. Where was I over budget? Where could I spend more?
  4. RResults We ended up winning a best-in-class award and achieved a grade of 96%.

Reflection

It was helpful in improving my research capabilities and tracking down the best attainable parts and prices. It demonstrated my ability to identify suitable components while documenting their specifications, availability, and cost.

Capstone

Work in progress

The Capstone is the largest project our team will develop. Our group's project focuses on the transliteration of movies and other media for EIDR (Entertainment Identifier Registry) using AI models. Our objective is to determine which AI/LLM is the most consistent and cost-effective.

  • Skills used: Document Writing, Note-taking
  • My role: Document Writer
  1. SSituation We are tasked with determining which AI/LLM is most effective at transliteration. Our client, Richard at EIDR, is seeking a solution that minimizes the amount of human involvement required for reviewing and approving media transliterations.
  2. TTask I am responsible for taking notes during weekly meetings as well as contributing to document writing and revision.
  3. AActions We determined that frontier AI models are the most effective candidates, but we are still evaluating which option provides the best balance between performance and cost. We are also continuing our research into cost analysis.

    4. Reflection

    This is my most important project, and it is teaching me about company workflows and standard operating procedures. It is also helping me develop effective note-taking skills and deepen my understanding of LLMs.
